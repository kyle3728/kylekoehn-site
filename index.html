Sophia

Sophia is my personal travel assistant. With my permission, it uses my Google Gmail and Calendar to help organize messages, travel plans, and schedules.

Kyle Media

Kyle Media is my toolkit for audiobooks, narrated tutorials, and personal music projects. It prepares narration and video, stores selected project files in a private Google Drive media workspace, and delivers finished audio to my YouTube account as private videos.

Each app has its own Google authorization and permissions. They share the privacy policy and terms of service on this site.
